# Owner guide: running your website (1 page)

**Edit the website:** go to **yourwebsite.co.uk/studio** and log in with your Sanity account.
**See your leads:** go to **yourwebsite.co.uk/admin** and enter your dashboard password.
After you click **Publish** in Studio, changes appear on the site within a few minutes.

---

### 1. Change the hero videos
Studio → **🏠 Homepage** → **Hero**
- **Video layout:** choose *1 video* or *3 videos (swipe)*.
- **Videos:** click a video → **Video file** → upload an MP4 (10–20 seconds, under ~8 MB, filmed on your own site). Add a **Poster image** (a still from the clip) so something shows while it loads.
- Change the **Headline**, **Subheadline** and the **button labels/links** in the same place → **Publish**.

### 2. Add a TikTok video to "Watch Us Build"
Studio → **🎬 TikTok gallery** → **+ Create**
1. **Title** and **Project type** (e.g. "Dormer loft, Northside", "Loft conversion").
2. **TikTok post link:** on TikTok open the video → **Share → Copy link** → paste it here.
3. Upload a **Thumbnail image** (a screenshot works) and/or the **Video file** (plays silently as a preview).
4. **Display order:** 1 = first. Tick **Hide from website** to hide it without deleting → **Publish**.
Clicking the tile on your site opens that exact TikTok post in a new tab.

### 3. Add a project / case study
Studio → **📁 Projects** → **+ Create** → fill in **Title**, **Area**, **The brief / The build / The finished space**, **Key facts** and **Photos** (first photo = cover; always add alt text describing the photo). Tick **Feature on homepage** for up to 3 projects. Delete the example projects once you have real ones.
For a before/after slider: **↔️ Before & after** → upload two photos taken from the **same angle**.

### 4. Edit any text
- **Phone numbers, email, WhatsApp, logo, colours, footer, announcement bar:** ⚙️ **Site settings**.
- **Services:** 🔨 **Services** (these also feed the quote form checklist automatically).
- **Reviews:** ⭐ **Reviews**. Paste real reviews word-for-word, first name + area only, and tick *"I confirm this is a real review"*. Only confirmed reviews appear in Google's review markup.
- **Trust claims** ("Fully insured", ratings, years trading, accreditations): ⚙️ **Site settings → Trust & reviews**. Every claim starts **off**. Only switch on what you can prove.
- **Privacy & cookies policy:** 🔒 **Privacy & cookies policy**. Fill in every gap, confirm the retention periods, and update **Last updated**.
- **FAQs, guides, cost guides, areas, team, About/Terms:** each has its own section in the left menu.
- **SEO:** every page has an **SEO** panel (title ≈ 55 characters, description ≈ 150 characters).
- Anything marked **[EDITABLE PLACEHOLDER]** or **[OWNER TO REVIEW BEFORE PUBLISHING]** still needs your real details. Search for "PLACEHOLDER" in Studio.

### 5. View and manage leads
**/admin** → enter password.
- **Hot / Warm / Cold** score is worked out from the answers marked ★ in Studio → Quote form → Answer options. Call hot leads first.
- **Partial** means they gave their name and number but didn't finish the form. Call them anyway.
- Change **Status** (New → Contacted → Survey booked → Won / Lost) and **Save**. Click a name to see every answer, their photos, and to add notes.
- **Export CSV** downloads the current list (respecting your filters) for Excel or Google Sheets.
- **Ask for a review:** copy the review link at the bottom of the dashboard and send it by text or WhatsApp after handover.
- **Data clean-up:** once a month, check the **Data clean-up** panel and delete enquiries past their retention period.
- **Settings to check:** if a yellow box appears at the top of the dashboard, pass it to your developer.
You also get an email for every new lead, with the score in the subject line.
